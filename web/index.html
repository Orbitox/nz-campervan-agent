Quick Jump (Days 1–28)

🚐 Certified Self-Contained Campervan Rules

  • Green Self-Containment Warrant: Ensure your rental campervan displays a valid NZ Green Self-Containment sticker with fixed toilet, fresh water (4L/person/day), and sealed grey/black waste tanks.
  • Max Daily Driving Limit (≤ 4.5 Hours): New Zealand highways are scenic, winding, and predominantly single-lane each way with one-lane bridges. Every day in this 28-day plan is paced at or below 4.3 hours of driving.
  • Prohibited Rental Roads (Insurance Void): Never drive a campervan onto Ninety Mile Beach (sand/tides), Skippers Canyon, Ball Hutt Road, or the steep Crown Range zig-zags (use State Highway 6 via Cromwell & Kawarau Gorge between Wanaka and Queenstown).

⛴️ Cook Strait Ferry (Day 13: Wellington → Picton)

  • Mandatory LPG Gas Shut-Off: Maritime New Zealand regulations require turning off your campervan LPG cylinder valve before boarding Interislander or BlueBridge ferries.
  • Check-In Window: Arrive at the Wellington Ferry Terminal at least 60 minutes before departure. Declare total vehicle length (e.g., 7.2m including rear bike racks).
  • Scenic Highlight: Spend the final 60 minutes of the 3.5-hour crossing on the upper observation deck as the ship glides through Tory Channel and Queen Charlotte Sound.

☀️ December Austral Summer Weather & Packing

  • Ultra-Long Daylight: Around the December 21 Summer Solstice, Auckland enjoys ~14.9 hours of daylight and Southland/Bluff enjoys nearly 16 hours (light until 10:00 PM!).
  • Seasonal Blooms: Look for crimson Pohutukawa ("New Zealand Christmas Trees") on North Island beaches and carpets of purple/pink Russell Lupins around Lake Tekapo, Lake Pukaki, and Wanaka.
  • Four Seasons in One Day: Even in December, pack a windproof Gore-Tex jacket, merino base layers for the Tongariro Alpine Crossing and Milford Sound, SPF50+ sunscreen, and picaridin sandfly repellent for the West Coast and Fiordland.
